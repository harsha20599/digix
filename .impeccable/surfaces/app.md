Scope: the Digix Android app, every screen. Mode: Operate, with onboarding allowed one authored moment.

Audience and job: Bluetooth watch owners worldwide. Set up once, dial codes from the wrist, open the app rarely.

Chosen direction: user-pinned after rejecting the dealt hand: "use apple design, simple, like shadcn design". Memorable moment: the first code dialed on the real watch makes the phone react live during onboarding.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The quietest possible app for something slightly magical. It refuses themed concepts, skeuomorphism, gradients and decorated cards: restraint is the identity, and motion carries the delight.

OWN-WORLD: Zinc neutral scale, near-white ground in light and zinc-950 in dark, 1dp hairline borders with no shadows, 12dp radius, near-black primary buttons, one green reserved for live. Inter with tabular numerals, Apple-style large titles and strong weight contrast. Lucide line icons. Spring physics on every state change, paired with haptics.

STORY: A person understands in one sentence that their watch can already do this, grants one permission with a plain reason, dials a real code, watches the phone respond, and trusts it after that.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: large title "Digix" top start, settings icon top end. Directly below, a bordered status row with a live dot, the words On or Off, one line of explanation, and the master switch at the end. Below it, the codes list in one bordered group: icon, action name, status line, and the code in tabular numerals at the end. The add action sits top end of the list heading.

FORM: User-pinned Apple and shadcn restraint, replacing the roll. seed:0fd7ad9ee03ff28e

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
